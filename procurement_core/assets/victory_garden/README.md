# Victory Garden Archival Plates (Public Domain)

All photographs in this directory are verified public-domain WWII archival works (1940–1945) converted to optimized WebP plates for `/victory-garden`:

1. `victory-garden-canada.webp` — *"Victory Gardens"* (Archives of Ontario, `I0040027`, Public Domain). Wartime staked Victory Garden plots adjacent to an Ontario industrial plant.
2. `rosemont-rubber-drive-1942.webp` — *"Brigade enfantine dans Rosemont - 1942"* (Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec / Montreal, 1942, Public Domain). Young Montrealers collecting bicycle and automobile tires and rubber footwear for the Canadian Armed Forces.
3. `scrap-metal-magnet-1942.webp` — *"Victory Program. Electro-magnet loads metal scrap"* (Library of Congress / FSA-OWI `8c34729v`, May 1942, Public Domain). Electro-magnet crane loading salvaged iron and steel scrap into a rail car for wartime furnaces.
4. `geco-canada-war-factory.webp` — *"Woman working at General Engineering Company (Canada)"* (Archives of Ontario, `I0058206`, Public Domain). Canadian munitions and precision-press operator at the GECO plant in Scarborough, Ontario.
