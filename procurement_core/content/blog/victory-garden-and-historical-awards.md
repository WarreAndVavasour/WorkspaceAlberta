Wouldn’t it be great if a small shop could see what Canada is actually buying this month — not just for what it already makes, but for what its existing machines and crews can pivot into next?

Today we are publishing four additions to **workspaceAlberta**, live at [elbowsupknivesout.warreandvavasour.com](https://elbowsupknivesout.warreandvavasour.com/) and over MCP at `https://elbowsupknivesout.warreandvavasour.com/mcp`.

## Victory Garden: existing tooling, urgent demand

Europe is settling into a long industrial conflict, and Canada is inside an urgent 18-month sprint across defence, Arctic logistics, energy resilience, and critical infrastructure. You cannot build ten crown mega-factories in eighteen months; you mobilize the welding bays, CNC shops, sheet-metal fabricators, modular builders, composite shops, electrical crews, and haulers that already have concrete floors and skilled people.

[Victory Garden](/victory-garden) (and the free `victory_garden_brief` MCP tool) takes any Canadian small business website URL, reads what the shop already builds, finds open **CanadaBuys** and **Alberta Purchasing Connection** tenders it can bid today, and uses TypeSafe Jev to identify adjacent public and sovereign demand lanes its existing equipment can redeploy into — paired with certification gates to verify and Canadian industrial retooling programs (PrairiesCan, BDC, ISED, NRCan).

## Historical contract awards and incumbent radar

Before an estimator spends a week on an RFP, the first question is practical: who won this buyer’s past similar contracts, and what did they actually pay?

`get_buyer_award_history` queries CanadaBuys fiscal-year award records and Alberta Purchasing Connection awarded notices to report disclosed minimum, median, and maximum CAD award values, repeat-winner incumbent concentration, and Alberta APC interested-supplier counts.

## Live trade-specific tender hubs and open agent discovery

Every open CanadaBuys and Alberta APC tender is now also organized into eight live trade pages at [/tenders](/tenders) — welding and structural steel, electrical, HVAC and plumbing, concrete and civil, general contracting, janitorial and facilities, heavy equipment and hauling, and IT and engineering — each paired with a clean Markdown feed (`.md`) and indexed in [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) and our enriched A2A Agent Card so search engines and AI assistants can cite live tenders directly.

## Free habit tools, Bid Packs, and any work email

Public search, daily briefs, and Victory Garden remain free with no account. Free sign-in now works with **Google or any work email address** (6-digit one-time code) and includes a saved shop profile, a 5-tender watchlist, deterministic bid/no-bid scorecards, and **2 free tender reviews**. When a larger RFP lands, shops can buy non-expiring one-time **Bid Packs** (C$19 for 1 tender or C$49 for 3 tenders) or **Pro Unlimited** (C$85/month CAD). See [pricing and terms](/pricing) and the [full tool guide](/features).

As always, workspaceAlberta finds, ranks, and breaks down public tenders. It never submits bids, contacts buyers, or guarantees awards. Verify eligibility, deadlines, and addenda directly on the official posting.
