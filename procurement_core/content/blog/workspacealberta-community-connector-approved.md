Wouldn’t it be great if finding public work started with a conversation?

Anthropic has approved workspaceAlberta as a **community connector** for the Claude directory. We’re pleased to share that milestone. Read the [connection guide](/support) for setup and visit the [directory listing](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/workspacealberta) for Claude.

## From a question to a useful opportunity

workspaceAlberta brings CanadaBuys and Alberta Purchasing Connection into your AI client. Ask for open opportunities, check approaching deadlines, inspect a tender and compare it with what your business actually does.

For example: “Find open Alberta construction opportunities closing in the next 30 days.” Results point back to the original postings so you can check the details and amendments yourself.

Public search and matching with an inline business profile work without an account. Sign in to save your own profile. Pro adds personal watchlists, bid/no-bid scorecards and tender document analysis. Signing in does not start a paid subscription; billing is handled separately.

## What the approval means

A community connector is screened for listing by Anthropic. It is a distinct category from a Verified connector and is not a certification or a security audit. Warre & Vavasour operates workspaceAlberta and is responsible for its tools and data handling. [Anthropic explains the connector labels here](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/verification).

The review also gave us useful feedback on sign-in declarations, tool descriptions, provider disclosures and processing times. Those details matter: people should understand which tools to choose, when they need an account and where their information goes.

## Built for the way you work

MCP is the practical connection between your AI client and these procurement tools. The service supports compatible clients and agent harnesses; the directory makes it easier for Claude users to discover it.

You can already [connect directly using the setup guide](/support). Read our [privacy notice](/privacy) before requesting model-assisted search or document review. Always verify eligibility, deadlines and requirements against the original tender. workspaceAlberta helps you assess an opportunity; it does not submit bids or contact buyers.

Built in Alberta. For what comes next.
