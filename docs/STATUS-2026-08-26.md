# WorkspaceAlberta — Status 2026-08-26

## What the product is

WorkspaceAlberta is a Canadian procurement intelligence workspace. Its core product is a
hosted MCP server (with a matching REST/OpenAPI surface) that unifies two public tender
sources — **CanadaBuys** (federal) and **Alberta Purchasing Connection** (provincial,
municipal, school boards, health entities, post-secondary) — into a single search surface.
It answers the practical questions a small Alberta shop asks: what work is open, which
opportunities fit what we do, what closes soon, and what to read first.

The hosted endpoint is live and free to browse:

```
https://elbowsupknivesout.warreandvavasour.com/mcp
```
