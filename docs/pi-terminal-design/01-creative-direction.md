# Warre & Vavasour — company homepage design direction

Planning pack, 8 September 2026. This pack specifies a Blender brand object and its proposed homepage behavior. Geometry, renders, animation exports, and website implementation are future work.

The homepage introduces Warre & Vavasour: custom AI tools for real work, based in Canmore and working across Western Canada. A small Pi-class object gives that work a physical form. Visitors bring it into focus, open it, look around it, and choose where to go next.

This is the company homepage. The deployed device's dual monitors, screen usage, harness, and workspace setup belong in deployment documentation. The object here is a company brand artifact, not a configuration diagram or a representation of everything included in an installation. The user's clarification governs this pack; existing terminal and homepage specifications remain unchanged.

Build one floating, open-frame object around a recognizably Pi-sized board. A thin silver base and open rectangular upper frame make it feel intentionally made. Muted green circuitry, charcoal connectors, a small finned cooler, and one steady amber indicator give it substance without visual noise. The proposed frame and storage layer are art direction, not an approved hardware design or a manufacturing specification.

Use a white void with no floor, contact shadow, horizon, or ambient camera travel. The object holds a stable pose. Its weightlessness comes from the surrounding field and its clean arrival and disappearance. Broad white reflections and small self-shadows explain the materials.

Keep the tone ephemeral, clean, quick, and smooth. Motion responds promptly and finishes decisively. Hover offers a small invitation; a deliberate click makes the object appear. A second action opens its layers. A visitor can leave at any moment without waiting for a sequence to finish.

The resting homepage contains the W&V mark, one sentence, one CTA, and the stage. Use the sentence “Custom AI tools for real work.” Use “Explore our work” for the CTA. Carry “Wouldn't it be great if...” into the later company and project conversations, where there is room to connect it to actual work. Do not add an audience claim, performance claim, price, client logo, or promised capability to the homepage.

The Pi is the visual invitation to the company's work. Route selection uses clear text labels beside the stage. A chip is not presented as the physical location of training or maps, and the device does not purport to run every service locally.

Use charcoal text on pure white, one familiar sans serif, and generous space. Set the mark as plain “W&V” in Arial Bold until an approved vector mark is supplied. Use Arial Regular for the sentence and controls. This is a specified temporary typographic treatment, not a new logo. Hardware surfaces carry no invented product wordmark or screen UI.

The first click resolves the field into the assembled object in 1.20 seconds and makes the four destination labels available within 0.24 seconds. Opening the object takes 1.60 seconds. Dismissal takes 0.48 seconds. A route can be selected while the visual is still resolving. Hover never triggers automatic disassembly.

Keep a readable empty pocket through the idle particle field. Once the object arrives, move the sentence and controls to a separate text zone so they do not compete with geometry. Use one quiet guide at a time. Home does not accumulate feature sections as visitors explore.

The art direction is fixed for this pack: one Pi-class object, floating pose, open frame, no display, no cable bundle, no monitor rig, and an assembly-based explode. Soldered parts remain attached to the board. The frame, cooler, board assembly, storage assembly, and base separate without turning into debris.

The technical reference is the behavior of Antigravity's field and morph components. Its ring, texture targets, and decisive easing inform the motion; the geometry, colors, object identity, navigation, and composition are ours. Source observations and unavailable references are recorded in [the reference notes](06-reference-notes-and-open-questions.md).

The pack is intended to be read in the following order. The mesh inventory and coordinates are authoritative in the Blender specification; the motion bible owns timing; the shot document owns filenames, camera registration, and sampling formats.

Approve the eventual first Blender proof by its smallest presentation: at 240 pixels wide, it should read as one deliberate piece of working hardware. At the exploded pose, the separate layers should be understandable without tiny labels. At every homepage state, the company sentence and a clear next action should remain easy to find.
