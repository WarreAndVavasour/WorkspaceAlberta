## Scope

A Statement of Work under AMSA to provide **Self-sustaining Application Services and Base Services**, focused on **Continuous Service Improvement and innovation** — specifically "employing new technology tools, **Automation-First and AI-Enabled Service Delivery**" — plus knowledge transfer to the Province.

## Why it fits Warre & Vavasour

- The SOW **explicitly names AI-enabled and automation-first delivery**, which is the shop's stated differentiator.
- It is a **services-and-innovation** engagement, not a commodity maintenance contract — the buyer is asking for new-tool adoption and efficiency gains.
- Sits in a **series** (Nos. 14, 15, 17 in the window), so winning the vehicle positions the shop for repeat SOWs.

## Risks / eligibility

- **AMSA-gated.** Must be on (or onboard into) the Application Master Services Agreement pre-qualified list.
- "Self-sustaining application services" leans toward ongoing support/ops; make sure the shop can staff steady-state support, not just project builds, or bid alongside a support-capable partner.
