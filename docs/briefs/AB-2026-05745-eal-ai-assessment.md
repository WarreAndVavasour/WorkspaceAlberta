## Scope

The Province wants the **design, development, testing, implementation, warranty support, and transition** of an **AI-assisted EAL Benchmark Assessment Application for Kindergarten to Grade 6**, to improve the **consistency, objectivity, and efficiency** of teacher-performed EAL proficiency assessments.

## Why it fits Warre & Vavasour

- It is a **custom AI application build** — design → build → test → deploy → support — exactly the shop's core service, not a licence resale or staff augmentation.
- The AI component is the point of the job (assisted scoring/benchmarking of language assessments), which is the kind of LLM/ML tooling the shop already builds.
- It is a **defined product with a clear user (teachers) and measurable outcome** (assessment consistency), so the proposal can be concrete rather than generic.
